# Most featured

Calculated by the hub, not by Claude.

A source is ★ Most featured when it is in the **top 5%** of sources by appearance count **and** has appeared in **at least 3 separate requests**.

- Counts: recommendations and books found in "top books" articles.
- Doesn't count: re-running the same request, or the user's own single lookups.
- Show independent endorsers alongside the count, since Claude tends to repeat itself.
