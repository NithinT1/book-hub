## How to use this
- Used for **Fit** (see the fit rule) and, for reading time, the Verdict. It never changes Quality, Timeless, honesty or evidence.
- "What I'm working on" lists goals and weak spots: a book that is the main book for one of them gets the highest Fit (label Self).
- "Values" picks biographies: people who lived the values the reader holds or wants to grow.
- "Subjects: Out" means Fit 0–2 unless the book is the main book for something they're working on.
- Sections not set: judge Fit from the question asked and the reader's library (what they've read, rated and noted).
