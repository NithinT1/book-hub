# Timeless test — non-fiction

**Main test: has it survived?** Serious readers recommended it decades ago and still recommend it today. Show the evidence (links) for both.

- **15 years or older:** verdict **Yes** or **No**, from the survival evidence.
- **Under 15 years:** verdict **Too new to call — promising** or **Too new to call — not**, judged on:
  - originality (foundational, a good blend of older ideas, or recycled),
  - density (each chapter adds something vs one idea stretched),
  - evidence (real research or real experience),
  - author credibility — this weighs more in fast-moving fields like AI, where most new books won't last.
- Blending old ideas well is fine (a modern, practical take on an ancient philosophy). One idea stretched over chapters is not.
- Always explain the verdict in 2–3 lines.

**Calibration (should come out Yes):** The Art of War, The Republic, On the Origin of Species, How to Win Friends and Influence People, The Elements of Style.
**Should come out No:** books whose one idea is stretched over hundreds of pages, or whose big claims have no evidence behind them.
