# Fit

Fit answers: **is this book right for this reader?** Read the reader's `nonfiction-profile` (or `fiction-profile` for fiction). Fit never changes Quality, Timeless, evidence or honesty.

Fit has two parts. **Self:** does it serve something the reader is working on (their goals and weak spots)? **General:** does it help them understand themselves or the world and connect ideas across fields? Self weighs more; both together is best.

| Fit | When | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | The main book for something they're working on, **and** strong understanding of the world | General + Self |
| 8 | The main book for something they're working on, or strong on it with some general value | Self |
| 6 | A must-know for understanding the world or themselves, in their subjects | General |
| 4 | Touches something they're working on, or useful but marginal in their subjects | — |
| 2 | Outside their subjects and not needed | — |
| 0 | A subject they marked "out", or clashes with their profile | — |

- Odd numbers are in between.
- Always give a one-line reason that names the goal or subject.
- **Fiction:** judge against the fiction profile and today's mood (ask 1–2 quick questions if the mood isn't clear and it matters).
- **Empty profile:** judge from the question asked only, and say once "Fit gets sharper if you fill in About me as a reader (Profile & settings)".
- Save with `save_card`: `fit` (0–10), `fit_label` (general_self · self · general · none), `fit_reason`. In `log_request`, pass the same number as `fit_score`.
