# Quality: fiction

Quality for novels and stories: **is this a good book?** About the book only. Mood, language and taste belong to Fit, never here.

## Gates
- on · **Not a book:** films and TV adaptations are mentioned on the card, never scored.

## Factors and weights
Score each factor 1–5. Higher weight = matters more to this reader.

| Factor | Weight | Question | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Craft | 4 | Prose, structure and characters | masterly | competent | clumsy |
| Pull | 4 | Does it make you keep reading? | can't put it down | steady | a chore |
| Depth | 4 | Does it say something true about people? | stays with you for years | some insight | nothing |
| Originality | 4 | A new voice, form or idea? | genre-defining | fresh within its genre | formula |
| Classic status | 4 | Has it lasted? | read for generations | well regarded for years | forgotten |

**Quality /10** = sum of (weight × factor) ÷ (5 × sum of weights) × 10, one decimal. Tiers as for non-fiction: Core 8.5+ · Strong 7.5–8.4 · Good 6.5–7.4 · Edge below 6.5.
- A book under about 15 years old scores 3 on Classic status ("too new to tell").
- Classic status is also shown on its own as **Timeless** (Yes / No / Too new), with evidence.
- Give a confidence label (High / Medium / Low).
